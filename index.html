Applied AI operating system

From experimentation to accountable execution.

The goal is not to maximize model use. It is to redesign the right work, use only the intelligence each task needs, and measure whether the result is worth the spend.

01

Map the work before adding AI

Decompose the process, identify decision points and constraints, and distinguish where agents, automation, or human judgment create the most value.

02

Route the task—not every task—to the best model

Balance accuracy, latency, context, privacy, and cost across frontier, specialized, and open-source models instead of defaulting to the largest option.

03

Govern consumption against business value

Classify approved use cases, set model and budget policies, observe token consumption, and evaluate quality and cost per successful outcome.

04

Build the operating model that makes adoption stick

Connect strategy, platform engineering, data rights, security, delivery, and change management so successful pilots can become durable production systems.
