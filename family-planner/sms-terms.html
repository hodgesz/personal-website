SMS Terms of Service

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Program and operator

Planner SMS is operated by Dakota Ridge Consulting LLC. It sends a verification code you request and reminders for calendar occurrences you explicitly select in Planner. It does not enroll other people, send marketing texts or change your existing Google alerts. SMS is pending provider verification and is not yet enabled.

Optional participation

You must be an authorized adult app user, own the destination phone and explicitly choose SMS before requesting a code. The consent checkbox starts unchecked. Phone verification does not automatically enable reminders; choose your preferences and individual events in the app. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of using other Planner features. Read the Privacy Policy and the enrollment steps.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with your verification requests and selected event reminders. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan.

Stop and get help

Reply STOP to stop messages from this sender. Planner suppresses the destination after a STOP request; replying START does not restore Planner consent or enroll reminders. You may also disable reminders or cancel pending enrollments in the app. A message already dispatched cannot be recalled.

Reply HELP for help, or contact me@jonathanhodges.ai for assistance, phone changes or privacy questions. This SMS program is not monitored as an emergency channel.

Delivery limits

Delivery depends on service and network availability, your permissions, event freshness and your settings. Quiet-hour reminders can be skipped. Planner does not catch up expired reminders or automatically resend a message with an uncertain outcome. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely on SMS for emergencies or time-critical safety notifications.
