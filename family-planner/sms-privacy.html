SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. This notice covers the optional Planner SMS program operated by Dakota Ridge Consulting LLC. For the separately authorized calendar integration, read the Google data and privacy notice.

Information and purpose

When you choose SMS, Planner uses your phone number, consent and verification state, reminder preferences, selected occurrence identifiers and delivery/opt-out history to verify your destination, schedule your requested reminders, enforce limits, prevent duplicates and honor STOP requests. The phone number and pending verification code are encrypted in the application database. A masked number is shown in app settings. Host administrators can access the installation and its backups.

Delivery providers

Twilio and the mobile carriers process the destination number and message content to deliver texts and return delivery or opt-out information. Planner sends a generic reminder time and instructions, rather than your event title, description, guest list or location. The public information website does not collect phone numbers; its hosting provider may process access metadata needed to serve these pages.

Sharing restrictions

We do not sell personal information or share it for third-party marketing or advertising. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

The marketing-sharing restriction does not prevent the limited processing by messaging providers needed to deliver the texts you request and enforce opt-outs. Your SMS consent applies to this program; it does not authorize unrelated uses by other apps sharing the operator’s Twilio account.

Retention and control

Disabling reminders or replying STOP prevents future eligible sends but does not erase retained delivery, spending, consent or suppression records. Records and backups can remain for opt-out enforcement and recovery; this installation does not promise a fixed retention period or automatic deletion. Contact the operator about access, correction, removal or a changed phone number. A request may need to preserve suppression information so that unwanted texts do not resume.

Reply HELP for help or STOP to stop. You can also disable reminders or cancel pending enrollments in Planner. For assistance and privacy questions, contact me@jonathanhodges.ai. See the SMS Terms of Service.
