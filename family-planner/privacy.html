Google data and privacy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. This notice describes the privately operated Family Planner integration, rather than a public calendar service.

Information accessed

With consent, Planner accesses the Google account identifier and verified email address, calendar-list metadata needed to identify and check access to selected calendars, and event information from those calendars. Event information can include title, description, location, dates and times, time zones, recurrence, cancellation status and provider identifiers and revisions.

The provider response can include organizer and attendee information, used to identify unsupported event edits. The normal imported event record retains an allowed set of fields; it does not retain arbitrary provider payloads or attendee lists.

Purpose and optional writes

Account identity identifies the connection. Calendar and event data support the household calendar, selected date coverage, synchronization, display privacy and recovery. If the adult grants event-write access and enables a writable destination, Planner sends requested ordinary-event creates, edits and deletes to Google. Planner does not read Gmail, send invitation responses automatically, sell calendar data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models.

Storage and who can see it

The household-operated installation stores imported event data and synchronization and operation history in its database. Refresh tokens are encrypted before storage. Authorized adults and paired household displays receive information according to the installation’s permissions and display settings. Administrators who operate the host can access its stored data and backups.

Google receives the API requests needed for the authorized integration. The Calendar integration does not send calendar data to the recipe service. This public information site does not connect to a Google account or request tokens; its hosting provider may process access metadata needed to serve these pages.

Retention and removal

Imported snapshots and history may remain to support outage viewing, synchronization and uncertain-operation recovery. Removing an event from Google can remove it from active Planner views after synchronization while cancellation and operation history remain. Revoking Google authorization does not itself delete already imported copies or backups.

You can review and revoke the application’s Google permission in Google Account connections. Contact the household administrator to remove locally stored information and address retained backups; this installation does not promise automatic deletion on disconnect or a fixed retention period.

Contact and changes

Contact the household administrator or the support email shown on the Google consent screen for access, removal or privacy questions. Update this notice when the integration’s actual data practices change.
