Family Planner · access and use

Private household access

Family Planner is operated for an invited household. This information site does not offer public registration or provide access to the household application.

Authorization

Use only accounts, calendars and devices you are authorized to connect. Each adult remains responsible for the Google permissions they grant and for the household’s calendar and shared-display choices. Google account authorization does not replace a Planner adult login.

Calendar behavior

Google Calendar remains the source of appointment data. Background refresh is subject to provider availability, authorization and the loaded date range. Read the freshness indicators; changes may take time to appear. Invitation appearance is governed by Google Calendar settings and is distinct from sending an RSVP.

Support

For access, correction, removal or support, contact the household administrator or the support email on the Google consent screen.
