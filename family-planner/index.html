Family Planner

A privately operated household planner for a shared calendar, responsibilities, meal planning and shopping lists.

This is an information site. Household access is arranged separately by the administrator; there is no public sign-up here.

How Google Calendar is used

An authorized adult connects a Google account and selects which calendars the household may see. Planner reads those calendars into a local view and refreshes the selected date range in the background.

Google Calendar remains the usual place to enter appointments and send invitations. When separately authorized and enabled, Planner can also create, edit and delete ordinary events on the selected writable calendar. It does not automatically RSVP to invitations or provide recurring-series or invitation editing.

Your account, your permission

The Google consent screen describes the requested access. Read-only access and event-write access are separate choices. Calendar scopes may cover more calendars than the subset selected in Planner; that distinction is explained during setup.

Read the privacy information for data use, storage, access controls and removal requests, and the access and use information.
