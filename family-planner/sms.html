Planner SMS reminders

Planner SMS is an optional app notification program operated by Dakota Ridge Consulting LLC. Invited adult app users can request a phone-verification code and choose reminders for their own selected calendar events. There are no marketing messages or public signup on this information site. SMS sending is pending provider verification.

How you choose SMS

  1. Sign in to your own adult Planner account and open My SMS reminders.
  2. Enter a phone number you own. Read the business identity, message description, frequency and rates notice, HELP/STOP instructions, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy beside the phone field.
  3. Choose the optional checkbox, which starts unchecked, then select Send verification code. Enter the code in Planner to verify your phone.
  4. Enable your reminder preferences and explicitly enroll each timed calendar occurrence you want. Verifying your phone alone does not select calendar reminders.

View a read-only capture of the actual consent screen. The live form is in the authenticated app; this public copy does not collect phone numbers.

Your control

Message frequency varies with your verification requests and selected events. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to stop. You can disable reminders or cancel pending enrollments in Planner. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase or of using the other app features.
