## Key Highlights

### Diverse Industry Experience
15+ years leading data teams across eCommerce, Healthcare, FinTech, EdTech, and Media
verticals, building SaaS-based AI and data products and leveraging analytics to optimize
internal business processes; developing and delivering on lucrative 0 to 1 data & AI
strategies for earlier-stage to Fortune 500 companies, accelerating business objectives and
finding efficiencies through automation.

### Deep Understanding of Generative AI
Enjoy working with companies and developing AI strategies and recipes that drive scalable
success, helping clients substantially reduce operational expenses by automating human
intensive processes such as customer support, tutoring/coaching, sales outreach, marketing,
software development, self-service analytics and more through AI autonomous agents and
RAG-based chatbots.

### True Servant Leader
Mentor who inspires confidence in employees by empowering them to do their jobs and
respecting them for their accomplishments, getting the most out of smaller to larger 100+
size teams of data professionals (data scientists, ML and data engineers, architects,
analysts), bringing value to organizations through AI and data monetization strategies and
execution.
